Built For Engineers & Power Users

Engineered Without Shortcuts

Most proximity tools fail because Bluetooth signal naturally flutters by ±15 dB from body-blocking. Presence was designed from mathematics up to prevent false locks.

Zero Cloud & 100% Offline

No cloud relays, no tracking telemetry, no Wi-Fi credentials, and no external dependencies. The Bluetooth advertisement never leaves the physical room.

No Auto-Unlock Guarantee

Security posture worth keeping: Presence locks your Mac on away, and informs you via local notification on return. It never bypasses your Touch ID or password.

Anti-Flap State Machine

Single-sample RSSI spikes never trip a lock. Decisions require consecutive confirmation counts and stale timeouts, fully verified by pure unit tests.

Short-Range Room Mode

In small apartments or hostel rooms, Bluetooth reaches through walls. One tap anchors the lock line ~10 dB under where you sit, locking within 1–2 meters.

Non-Connectable Beacon

Standard Bluetooth pairing stops RSSI reports. Presence uses non-connectable BLE beacons with AllowDuplicates to stream 10 Hz continuous signal updates.

CLI & Diagnostic Tooling

Ships with PresenceCLI for real-time terminal RSSI dumps, histogram visualizers, and state transitions before trusting it with your screen.

Architecture

How Presence Coordinates in Real Time

A seamless pipeline from phone hardware advertisement to low-level macOS session control.

01 • Android FGS

Beacon Service

Foreground service advertises non-connectable UUID at ~10 Hz with low battery impact.

02 • CoreBluetooth

Scanner Engine

macOS scans with AllowDuplicates to collect continuous per-packet RSSI numbers.

03 • Pure Logic

State Machine

Debounces noise, handles body-blocking shifts, and requires 3 confirmations or stale timeout.

04 • macOS Lock

Session Lock

Calls CGSession or ScreenSaver lock to instantly suspend the active session.

3-Minute Setup

Get Up and Running

No server setup, no terminal commands required unless you want to calibrate manually.

1

Install Beacon on Android Phone

Phone Companion

Download and install Presence-Android.apk. Grant Bluetooth and Notification permissions, tap "Start beacon", then tap Copy UUID.

UUID: 3F9E2A84-17BD-49F1-8B6C-7A9B32015842 Example Identity
2

Launch Menu Bar App on Mac

macOS App

Download Presence-macOS.zip, extract Presence.app, and drag it into /Applications. Open it and allow Bluetooth permissions when prompted.

Open the menu-bar Presence icon → Settings → Identity → paste your phone's UUID.

3

One-Click Calibration (Short-Range Mode)

Tuning

Sit naturally at your desk with your phone. In the menu-bar app, navigate to Settings → Tuning and click:

"Set boundary where I'm sitting now"

The lock threshold instantly anchors ~10 dB below your current sitting signal. When you walk 1–2 meters away, your Mac secures itself automatically.
