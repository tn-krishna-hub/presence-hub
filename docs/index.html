Core Capabilities

Built For Engineers & Privacy Purists

Engineered specifically to solve the limitations of standard Bluetooth proximity tools.

01

Zero Cloud • Zero Telemetry

No external relays, no accounts, no Wi-Fi credentials required. All Bluetooth Low Energy advertisement stays completely local to your room.

02

No Auto-Unlock Stance

Security policy worth keeping: Presence securely locks when you step away, and sends an optional desktop alert when you return. It never bypasses your macOS password.

03

Anti-Flap Confirmation Logic

Human bodies absorb ~15 dB of Bluetooth signal. Presence utilizes a unit-tested debounced state machine so you never get locked out while sitting and typing.

04

Hostel & Small Space Mode

In apartments or dorms, Bluetooth penetrates drywall easily. One-tap calibration anchors your lock threshold just 1–2 meters from your chair.

05

Non-Connectable Beacon

Unlike standard Bluetooth pairing which throttles RSSI updates, Presence utilizes continuous non-connectable broadcast with AllowDuplicates at ~10 Hz.

06

CLI Diagnostics Tooling

Includes PresenceCLI for real-time terminal signal dump, RSSI histograms, and state testing before trusting it with your screen.

Pipeline

Under the Hood

How packets travel from Android hardware to the macOS session controller.

Phase 1

Android Foreground Service

BeaconService broadcasts custom UUID via BLE Advertiser with low power consumption.

Phase 2

CoreBluetooth Scanner

macOS receives ~10 Hz continuous RSSI packets without pairing requirement.

Phase 3

Presence State Machine

Applies moving average filter, confirms consecutive away samples, and validates timeouts.

Phase 4

Lock Controller

Executes CGSession -suspend or screen-saver engine to protect the Mac.

Quickstart

Up and Running in 3 Steps

Everything you need to pair and configure Presence.

1

Start the Beacon on Your Android Phone

Android Companion

Install Presence-Android.apk, grant Bluetooth and Notification permissions, tap "Start beacon", and tap "Copy UUID".

2

Paste Identity in macOS Menu-Bar App

macOS App

Extract Presence-macOS.zip into /Applications, launch it, click the menu-bar icon → Settings → Identity, and paste your UUID.

3

Calibrate Sitting Boundary

1-Click Tuning

Sit naturally at your desk with your phone in hand or pocket. In menu-bar settings, tap "Set boundary where I'm sitting now". The threshold anchors safely ~10 dB below your desk signal.
